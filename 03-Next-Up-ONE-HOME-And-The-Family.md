# Next up: ONE HOME and the family

**Category:** Coming next  
**Summary:** A first look at the shared identity taking shape across our homework, early-years, primary and sixth-form services.

The ONE EDUCATE refresh marks the beginning of a wider design update across OneLearn.

**ONE HOME is next for interface mockups.** Its purple identity will stay, paired with the new people-centred logo. The design work will focus on making homework easy to find, understand and follow, while giving staff a clear place to set work and review progress.

We’re also introducing new logo designs for three other members of the family:

- **OnePrimary:** pale blue, with a calm and welcoming identity for primary schools.
- **OneNursery:** warm yellow, reflecting early years, children and the families who support them.
- **OneSixth:** muted rose, giving sixth-form students and staff a more mature look.

The plan is to bring the clarity of ONE EDUCATE to these services while keeping each one suited to its setting. A nursery, a primary school and a sixth form have different needs, and their workspaces should reflect that.

These logos are a first look. The wider interface updates are still ahead, and the ONE HOME mockups will be our next design step. We’ll share previews and progress on OneBlog before announcing releases.

One family, with a distinct place for every stage of education.
