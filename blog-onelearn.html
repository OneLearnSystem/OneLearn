A simpler home for OneLearn

More space, clearer choices and a connected product family.

OneLearn team · 3 October 2026

We’re giving OneLearn a fresh look, starting with a more minimal homepage and a new logo inspired by people learning together.

The latest design preview puts the essentials first. A simple introduction leads straight to the product family, with clear descriptions of what each service does. There’s more breathing room, fewer competing elements and an easier route to the service you’re looking for.

ONE EDUCATE and ONE HOME take their place alongside OnePrimary, OneNursery and OneSixth. Each keeps its own colour and purpose while sharing a more recognisable family identity.

OneBlog is staying. It will remain the place to explore announcements, first looks and the thinking behind our updates. OneStatus also keeps a clear place in the navigation for service information and planned maintenance.

The new direction is about making OneLearn easier to explore—from discovering a product to finding your portal or getting in touch about your school.

This is a design preview, and the refreshed website has not launched yet. We’ll share more as it moves from concept to a working site.

More learning. Less juggling. That’s the direction we’re taking.

There’s more to come.
