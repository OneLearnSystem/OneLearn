# Meet ONE EDUCATE

**Category:** Product update  
**Summary:** A new name, a warmer identity and clearer workspaces for the people who keep a school moving.

OneEducation is becoming **ONE EDUCATE**. Our redesigned interface has been built and is ready for rollout, bringing a fresh green-and-amber identity to the school management system.

At the centre of the refresh is a simple idea: different school roles need different starting points.

Administrators get a school overview with attendance summaries, register follow-up and clear access to management tools. Teachers get a workspace focused on their lessons, registers and everyday actions. HUB and pastoral staff get a support dashboard with relevant On-Call alerts, lesson removals and scheduled support arrangements.

The student and family portal has a matching refresh, bringing lessons, homework and school notices into a calmer layout. The new look also carries through the existing registers, timetables, forms and feature pages.

The familiar tools remain part of the system, including attendance, classes, points, detentions, exams and school resources. Existing permissions continue to determine who can see and use each area.

Our new people-centred logo reflects the purpose behind ONE EDUCATE: **New Education Through Hope**.

We’re excited to share this next chapter. Follow OneBlog for rollout news and further previews from across the OneLearn family.
